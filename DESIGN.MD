## Brand & Style

This design system embodies elevated quiet luxury, translating the meditative atmosphere of architectural retreat centers into a calm digital experience. The interface deliberately departs from aggressive athletic tropes, high-intensity color bursts, and hyper-metricized fitness dashboards. Instead, it embraces organic luxury, mindfulness, and scholarly restraint.

The aesthetic marries classical editorial structure with gentle, welcoming humanist geometry. Generous negative space, rhythmic typographic pacing, and subtle tactile cues induce a physical sense of decompression. Visual compositions utilize deliberate spatial balance, asymmetrical natural arrangements, soft diffused light, and grounding earth tones to invite focus, breath, and intentional movement.

## Colors

The color palette is built on mineral clays, bleached linens, and sun-warmed plaster.

- **Primary (`#C95632`):** Sunbaked terracotta. Used intentionally for focused focal points, high-priority interactive cues, and delicate signature accents without overwhelming the canvas.
- **Secondary (`#6B655F`):** Muted stone charcoal. Provides soft grounding for supporting content, secondary interactive states, and metadata, avoiding high-contrast black harshness.
- **Tertiary (`#EAE3D9`):** Pressed linen sand. Functions as an alternate container surface and subtle structural framing, establishing soft visual stratification between tiers.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F6`):** Soft alabaster parchment. The foundational canvas surface that eliminates harsh stark white glare.

### Semantic Tones
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F9F8F6`
- **Surface Elevated (Cards):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Subtle (Muted Tiers):** `#F2ECE4`
- **Text Primary:** `#2D2A26` (Deep botanical charcoal)
- **Text Secondary:** `#6B655F` (Softened earth grey)
- **Text Tertiary / Muted:** `#9C948A`
- **Structural Border:** `#E6DFD5`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical intellectual cadence with modern clarity.

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Channels scholarly balance, timelessness, and poetic gravity. Used across all editorial markers, session titles, and narrative headers. For focal, emotive terms (such as "Center", "Practice", or "Breathe"), integrate the true italic variant of EB Garamond (`editorial-italic`) seamlessly within headings to build editorial texture.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a crisp, warm, humanist counterbalance. Its subtle rounded features maintain friendliness without sacrificing high legibility across class itineraries, duration indicators, and wellness guides.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines preserve tighter tracking at display scales for editorial poise, while upper-case category labels and metadata utilize deliberate, wide tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) for quiet elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an expansive, breathing grid system calibrated around visual rest rather than visual density.

- **Grid Model:** Desktop views employ a 12-column layout with 2rem (32px) gutters and max-width containers constrained to 1320px to prevent visual fatigue. Mobile interfaces collapse cleanly into a 4-column flow with 1rem (16px) gutters.
- **Negative Space:** Sections must be partitioned with unhurried vertical rhythm. Hero sections, quote highlights, and thematic transitions use `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps to instill mindfulness.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Visual interest is achieved by placing organic imagery and focal text offset across columns (e.g., an 8-column editorial lead anchored by a 4-column quiet reflection preview).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft ambient warmth, tonal planes, and translucent glass diffusion rather than sharp, industrial drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#F9F8F6`. Content islands lift gently into `#FFFFFF` cards, while secondary informational panels recede subtly into `#F2ECE4` or `#EAE3D9`.
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** When cards float or hover, shadows utilize low-opacity terracotta and raw umber tints instead of pure neutral greys.
  - *Resting card shadow:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(107, 101, 95, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(107, 101, 95, 0.03)`
  - *Elevated focus shadow:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(201, 86, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04)`
- **Glassmorphism:** Badges, navigation bars, and session overlays feature high-translucency white surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) backed by a 16px to 24px backdrop blur, bordered by a delicate 1px highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`).
- **Geometric Depth:** Gentle, circular tinted silhouettes (in faint terracotta washes, e.g., `rgba(201, 86, 50, 0.06)`) rest underneath portrait and pose photography, introducing organic layering.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces softness and rounded continuity, mirroring biological contours and fluid movement.

- **Pill System (`roundedness: 3`):** Interactive controls, badges, and action triggers default to full pill profiles (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), reinforcing physical softness.
- **Card Enclosures:** Containers adopt oversized, gentle corners (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem) to soften content perimeters and sustain a sanctuary-like calmness.
- **Organic Cutouts:** Circular avatars and circular geometric backdrop accents emphasize cyclical continuity and wholeness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid charcoal pill (`#2D2A26` background, `#F9F8F6` text) with internal padding of `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, softens into a warm deep tint with subtle shadow lift.
- **Accent Action:** Solid terracotta pill (`#C95632` background, `#FFFFFF` text), reserved for direct commitments (e.g., "Begin Practice").
- **Ghost / Outline:** Pill-shaped transparent button bordered with 1px `#E6DFD5`, primary charcoal text. On hover, transitions background to `#F2ECE4`.

### Cards & Content Pods
- **Practice & Class Cards:** White base (`#FFFFFF`) with 1.75rem rounded corners and a 1px border of `#E6DFD5`. Internal padding set to `1.75rem`. Features warm resting ambient shadows. Image containers inside have `1.25rem` corner radius and natural matte finishing.
- **Quote & Reflection Tiles:** Background `#F2ECE4` or `#EAE3D9`, minimal or zero outline, accompanied by display typography and expansive inner padding (`2.5rem`).

### Badges & Chips
- **Category & Duration Chips:** Pill shaped, styled as glassmorphic badges (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` background, 12px blur, `#E6DFD5` border) or linen fills (`#F2ECE4`). Text in `label-xs` uppercase tracking (`0.06em`).

### Input Fields
- **Fields:** Fully rounded pill inputs with soft cream background (`#FFFFFF`), subtle border (`#E6DFD5`), and deep charcoal text. Floating focus states exchange the border for a 1px terracotta stroke (`#C95632`) accompanied by a faint `rgba(201, 86, 50, 0.12)` outer halo.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- **Controls:** Smooth circles for radio indicators and rounded squares (`0.375rem`) for checkboxes. Inactive borders use `#D6CEC3`. Active states fill with `#C95632` exhibiting a clean white glyph.

### Audio & Session Players
- **Player Bar:** Floating glassmorphic dock anchored to bottom viewport (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, backdrop blur 20px, border `#E6DFD5`). Minimal scrubbers filled with `#C95632` on `#EAE3D9` track.
